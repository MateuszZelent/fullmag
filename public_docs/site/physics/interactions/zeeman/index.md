# Zeeman interaction

The Zeeman interaction couples magnetization to a prescribed external magnetic field. Fullmag
authors the public field as magnetic flux density $\mathbf B_{\mathrm{ext}}$ in tesla and
converts it once to $\mathbf H_{\mathrm{ext}}$ in amperes per metre.

(physics-zeeman-problem-statement)=
## Physical problem

The prescribed field is external input and is not solved self-consistently. This page separates
authored semantics, planner resolution, executable backend lanes, and canonical observable
boundaries across uniform `Zeeman`, spatially varying `StaticFieldMap`, and localized time-varying
`RegionalFieldDrive` / `MaskAntenna` excitations.

(physics-zeeman-governing-equations)=
## Governing equations

```{math}
:label: eq-public-zeeman-zeeman-conversion
\mathbf H_{\mathrm{ext}}=\frac{\mathbf B_{\mathrm{ext}}}{\mu_0}.
```

```{math}
:label: eq-public-zeeman-zeeman-energy
E_{\mathrm Z}
=
-\mu_0\int_{\Omega_m}\mathbf M\cdot\mathbf H_{\mathrm{ext}}\,\mathrm dV
=
-\int_{\Omega_m}\mathbf M\cdot\mathbf B_{\mathrm{ext}}\,\mathrm dV .
```

The corresponding effective field is exactly the prescribed field:

```{math}
:label: eq-public-zeeman-zeeman-field
\mathbf H_{\mathrm Z}=\mathbf H_{\mathrm{ext}}.
```

A positive field lowers the energy of magnetization aligned with it. Reversing
$\mathbf B_{\mathrm{ext}}$ reverses the field and the signed energy. For localized time-varying
excitations (`RegionalFieldDrive`, `MaskAntenna`), the drive field $\mathbf H_{\mathrm{drive}}(\mathbf x, t) = \mathbf B_{\mathrm{drive}}(\mathbf x, t)/\mu_0$ enters $\mathbf H_{\mathrm{eff}}$ additively while preserving separate energy and field observables (`H_drive`, `E_drive`).

(physics-zeeman-assumptions-and-validity)=
## Assumptions and validity

`Zeeman` is a prescribed-field interaction. It does not solve Maxwell equations, inductive
coupling, eddy currents, or the field generated by transport current. A zero field is legal.
Spatial maps (`StaticFieldMap`) and localized/RF drives (`RegionalFieldDrive`, `MaskAntenna`,
`MicrostripAntenna`, `CPWAntenna`, `AntennaFieldSource`, and `fm.space_time` waveforms) are
separate field-source contracts and must not be silently collapsed into a uniform constant vector.
At the observable validation boundary (`crates/fullmag-plan/src/validate.rs`), requesting `H_ext`
or `E_ext` requires a uniform `Zeeman(...)` term, whereas requesting `H_drive` or `E_drive`
requires an active `RegionalFieldDrive` or `MaskAntenna` drive.

(physics-zeeman-discrete-realization)=
## Backend capability matrix and GPU kernel execution

| Solver | Device | Authoring / IR | Executable realization | Scientific qualification | Exact boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FDM | CPU | `Zeeman(B)`, `StaticFieldMap`, `RegionalFieldDrive`, `MaskAntenna` | implemented (`external_field_add_into_soa`, `regional_field_drives_add_into_soa_at_time`) | analytic sign, scaling, and drive-envelope tests | uniform field, per-cell static map, and time-varying regional/mask drives on active cells |
| FDM | GPU | same canonical intent | implemented CUDA path | precision-specific CPU/GPU parity | device-resident uniform, static-map, and regional-drive buffers; residency recorded in provenance |
| FEM | CPU | `Zeeman(B)`, `RegionalFieldDrive`, `MaskAntenna` | implemented (`initialize_uniform_zeeman_field`, `materialize_regional_field_drive`) | mesh-independent uniform-field and regional-drive oracles | `StaticFieldMap` rejected on FEM; uniform and regional drives resolved on magnetic nodes |
| FEM | GPU | same FEM canonical intent | implemented CUDA path (`fullmag_cuda_external_energy_blocks`, `gpu_regional_field_drive_materialize_and_accumulate`, `fullmag_cuda_accumulate_heff_fused_xyz`) | executed-device parity required | strict GPU execution with no host fallback; single-launch fused `H_eff` accumulation, energy-reduction gating on RK substeps, and lazy `h_drive_zeroed` memset skip when all regional drives are inactive |

On FEM GPU time-integration steps, `fullmag_cuda_accumulate_heff_fused_xyz` merges uniform Zeeman
and active regional-drive contributions directly into $\mathbf H_{\mathrm{eff}}$ in a single kernel
launch, while `gpu_regional_field_drive_materialize_and_accumulate` tracks a lazy `h_drive_zeroed`
flag to avoid redundant zero-fills during inactive drive windows, and
`fullmag_cuda_external_energy_blocks` runs only when scalar energy output is requested (see
{ref}`numerical-methods-performance-fem-gpu-kernels`).

### Stage-first example

```python
# %% Study, execution lane, and magnetic body
import fullmag as fm

nm = 1.0e-9
study = fm.study("zeeman_reference")
study.engine("fdm")
study.device("cpu", precision="double")
study.mode("strict")
study.objects.mesh.defaults(cell_size=(2 * nm, 2 * nm, 2 * nm))
body = study.geometry(fm.Box(40 * nm, 20 * nm, 4 * nm), name="film")
body.Ms = 8.0e5
body.Aex = 13.0e-12
body.alpha = 0.02
body.m = fm.texture.uniform(1.0, 0.0, 0.0)

# %% Uniform Zeeman field in tesla and physical-time precession stage
study.exchange()
study.b_ext(0.0, 0.0, 0.1)  # tesla
study.stages.add_run(stage_id="precession", until=1.0e-12)
```

For exact constructor arguments and type validation on `fm.Zeeman`, `fm.StaticFieldMap`,
`fm.RegionalFieldDrive`, and `fm.MaskAntenna`, see {doc}`/python-api/interactions/zeeman`.

(physics-zeeman-validation)=
## Validation boundary

`Zeeman.__init__` uses `as_vector3` in `packages/fullmag-py/src/fullmag/_validation.py` to validate
vector length and float conversion, while finite-value rejection (`NaN` and infinity), duplicate
`Zeeman` detection, and canonical observable gating (`H_ext`/`E_ext` versus `H_drive`/`E_drive`)
are enforced downstream at the `ProblemIR` and planner validation boundary.

(physics-zeeman-problem-ir)=
## ProblemIR and observables

```json
{"kind": "zeeman", "B": [0.0, 0.0, 0.1]}
```

Expected observables for uniform `Zeeman` are `B_ext` in tesla, `H_ext` in $\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$,
and `E_ext` in joules, keeping the one-time $\mathbf B/\mu_0$ conversion explicit. Localized
`RegionalFieldDrive` and `MaskAntenna` excitations report `H_drive` in $\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$ and
`E_drive` in joules separately and do not overwrite `H_ext` or `E_ext`.

## Required numerical validation

- exact vector conversion $\mathbf H=\mathbf B/\mu_0$;
- uniform macrospin energy for parallel, perpendicular, and antiparallel states;
- odd symmetry under field reversal;
- zero-field exact zero contribution;
- time-dependent precession frequency against the selected LLG $\gamma$ convention;
- regional-drive activation windows, zero-fill consistency, and `H_drive`/`E_drive` separation;
- matched CPU/GPU field and energy comparisons.

(physics-zeeman-round-trip-and-failure-semantics)=
## Round-trip and failure semantics

Requested intent preserves the authored induction vector `B` in tesla, static map or regional-drive
metadata, target references, and execution request (`engine`, `device`, `precision`, `mode`) across
Python authoring, UI editing, and canonical `ProblemIR`. Resolved execution records the converted
$\mathbf H_{\mathrm{ext}}$ vector in $\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}$ and the selected backend, precision,
and discretization provenance. Validation errors reject malformed vector lengths, duplicate
`Zeeman` declarations, mismatched observable requests (`H_ext`/`E_ext` without `Zeeman`, or
`H_drive`/`E_drive` without a regional/mask drive), and non-finite components before execution.
Unsupported combinations (such as `StaticFieldMap` on FEM, single precision on FEM time-domain
lanes, or unavailable strict GPU execution) fail closed and are never silently omitted, downgraded
to CPU, or converted into another field source.

## Implementation mapping

Python owns authoring and serialization (`packages/fullmag-py/src/fullmag/model/energy.py`,
`packages/fullmag-py/src/fullmag/model/antenna.py`,
`packages/fullmag-py/src/fullmag/model/mask_field_drive.py`,
`packages/fullmag-py/src/fullmag/world.py`), `ProblemIR` owns canonical intent, planners
(`crates/fullmag-plan/src/fdm.rs`, `crates/fullmag-plan/src/fem.rs`,
`crates/fullmag-plan/src/validate.rs`) convert $\mathbf B_{\mathrm{ext}}/\mu_0$ once and enforce
lane and observable legality, and backend CPU/CUDA kernels (`crates/fullmag-engine/src/fdm/cpu/fields/zeeman.rs`,
`backends/fem/cpu/mfem/interactions/zeeman_uniform_field.cpp`,
`backends/fem/cpu/mfem/interactions/zeeman_regional_field.cpp`,
`backends/fem/gpu/cuda/interactions/zeeman/zeeman_kernels.cu`,
`backends/fem/gpu/cuda/interactions/zeeman/regional_field_kernels.cuh`,
`backends/fem/gpu/cuda/fields/vector_field_kernels.cu`) own uniform nodal or cell-wise evaluation,
regional-drive materialization, and energy integration. Control Room exposure for field-source
parameters is listed in {doc}`/frontend/capability-register`; parameters without a UI control are
configured in Python.

(physics-zeeman-limitations)=
## Limitations

Spatial field maps (`StaticFieldMap`) are currently FDM-only and fail closed on FEM. Time-dependent
RF/regional excitations (`RegionalFieldDrive`, `MaskAntenna`, `AntennaFieldSource`) are separate
typed IR contracts rather than overloaded variants of `Zeeman(B)`. Every dynamic field source must
state whether it is sampled at stage start, every accepted step, or every RHS evaluation.
